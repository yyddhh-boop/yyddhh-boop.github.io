About

Structures, concepts, and physical meaning

I am a researcher in mathematical and condensed-matter physics. My interests include categorical and homotopical foundations of physics, higher gauge theory, type theory and formalization, lattice gauge systems, and AI-assisted scientific research.

I am especially interested in a broad operational question: how do intelligent systems acquire, stabilize, communicate, and use mathematical concepts—and what changes when some of those systems are artificial?
